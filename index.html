About Pure Brokers

Built by people who've seen the other side.

For over a decade, our founder worked in the trenches of the automotive industry: dealerships, finance offices, and insurance desks. He saw firsthand how buyers get squeezed by hidden fees, confusing terms, pressure tactics, and insurance quotes that feel like punishment.
